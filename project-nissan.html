Nissan Latam

UX DesignUI DesignAutomotiveAI ChatbotFigma

Context

Nissan is one of the biggest Japanese car manufacturers. Their family of automobiles includes brands like Infiniti, Datsun and different tuning products under the label Nismo. It also has a strong presence in Latin America, a car market that is growing exponentially and represents a great business opportunity.

Challenge

Nissan Latam is in search of a way to optimize their internal process of quote and requesting autoparts from providers. On the user's side, the telematics app has proven to offer value to customers but its low feedback count on stores drives potential users away.

Goal

Develop a platform enhanced with A.I. that automates the search for autoparts and stores, and registers all of this information in a database. Research a way of improving the user willingness to rate the app on the stores.

This first version implements in its core an advanced artificial intelligence. This A.I. is enabled for live interaction via chat and offers precise answers to user requests and an extra feature: capacity to upload and process documents and data.

The design and programming team have also taken a close look at the possible interactions of A.I. and humans to ensure this product's safety. There are established limits for what information can be retrieved, what requests are acceptable and how to handle the user's frustrations in what-if scenarios.

Design Process

Understanding the Market

We audited Nissan's digital touchpoints across 6 LATAM markets — identifying inconsistencies in service booking flows, language gaps, and disconnected dealer systems.

  • Competitor benchmarking across 8 automotive brands
  • Stakeholder interviews with regional market leads
  • Heuristic evaluation of the existing platform

User Journey Mapping

We conducted 24 interviews with vehicle owners in Mexico, Brazil and Argentina — mapping the emotional journey from service alert to vehicle pickup.

  • 24 user interviews across 3 markets
  • Journey maps & pain point prioritization
  • 3 user archetypes defined

IA & Chatbot Flows

We defined the information architecture for the app and AI chatbot — mapping 30+ conversation trees and service booking flows.

  • 30+ chatbot conversation flows architected
  • Mid-fi wireframes validated with 12 users
  • 3 iterative sprint cycles before sign-off

Visual Design & Handoff

Final UI built on Nissan's brand system. Delivered 120+ screen designs and a Figma component library across 6 language variants.

  • 120+ screens across 6 languages
  • Component library with design tokens
  • Figma handoff + QA support

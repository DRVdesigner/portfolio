La Calera

Offline-First Mobile App UX Research Figma

Context

La Calera is one of the largest agricultural companies in Peru, specializing in egg production at scale. The company is redirecting its focus toward automating internal farm processes, where record-keepers and veterinaries still carry out their duties the old-fashioned way: by hand on paper.

Challenge

Goal

Develop a platform that allows farm workers to efficiently capture and upload egg production data — even without a stable network connection.

Project Overview

La Calera is one of the largest agricultural companies in Peru, specializing in egg production at scale. The company is redirecting its focus toward automating internal farm processes, where record-keepers and veterinaries still carry out their duties the old-fashioned way: by hand on paper.

Many of these farms are located in areas with no access to cellular networks, generating a critical problem: uploading recorded data to cloud servers is a mandatory and key step in the farm daily workflow, yet connectivity cannot be guaranteed.

Develop a platform that allows farm workers to efficiently capture and upload egg production data — even without a stable network connection.

Design Process

Record Keeper

Responsible for the daily data capture of egg production in each laying module. Works directly on the farm floor, registering metrics manually — often in areas with zero network coverage.

Veterinary

Monitors the health and welfare of the laying hens. Documents clinical observations, treatment logs, and mortality data — requiring reliable data entry tools that function offline.

The production of eggs is usually asymmetrical. Different factors intervene in its development — the health condition of the hen, the nest cleanliness, and aesthetic anomalies that render the egg not eye-appealing to consumers. We have categorized them with the following labels:

Good state Double yolk Porous Dirty Broken Fárfara Bad formation White

The visual system was built around clarity and legibility in harsh field conditions. A constrained palette of three colors, a single typeface, a custom icon set, and a lean component library kept the interface consistent across both user flows — and reduced cognitive load for workers with limited screen time.

Screens

Record Keeper

The Record Keeper flow is built for speed and reliability under field conditions. Each daily session covers one laying module at a time — capturing egg counts by category, flagging anomalies, and queuing data for sync the moment connectivity is restored.

Screens

Veterinary

The Veterinary flow prioritizes clinical precision. Observations are tied to specific hens and modules, with structured fields for treatment logs, mortality records, and health status — all stored locally and uploaded when the farm network is available.

Orion by Google

DashboardUX ArchitectureUI KitIllustrationsBranding

Context

Orion is a B2B broker platform from Google that allows carriers to buy internet (Wi-Fi) from different venues. It enhances the connectivity experience for customers in areas where the cellular network is poor and allows local businesses to generate profits by selling their Wi-Fi.

Challenge

Orion's platform is already functioning and has been released to other businesses with great adoption. The issue lies in the lack of information architecture, visual consistency, use of specific component libraries and better data visualization.

Goal

Improve the UX experience in the main broker platform and create a marketing website whose brand identity is closely related to the average Google look and feel.

Project Overview

Orion is a B2B broker platform from Google that allows carriers to buy internet (Wi-Fi) from different venues. It enhances the connectivity experience for customers in areas where the cellular network is poor and allows local businesses to generate profits by selling their Wi-Fi.

Orion's platform is already functioning and has been released to other businesses with great adoption. The issue lies in the lack of information architecture, visual consistency, use of specific component libraries and better data visualization.

Improve the UX experience in the main broker platform and create a marketing website whose brand identity is closely related to the average Google look and feel.

Design Process

Business Model

Orion Wi-Fi lets mobile devices connect to premium Wi-Fi while connection quality, security and policies are handled automatically — no extra setup required for the end user.

  • Seamless premium Wi-Fi for mobile users
  • New revenue streams for participating venues
  • Hotels, airports, conference centers, stadiums and malls

Architecture

The platform splits into two coordinated sides: Supply (venues selling Wi-Fi access) and Demand (carriers buying connectivity). Each side has its own architecture and entry points.

Supply

The supplier-side flow — venues integrate their Wi-Fi networks and earn revenue per connected session.

Demand

The buyer-side flow — telecom carriers connect their subscribers to premium Wi-Fi through Orion's broker layer.

Showcase

Supply

Owners of supply venues are able to manage multiple of them at the same time with Orion. Monitor the amount of devices connected to them, check the connection stability and visualize the money generated.

Showcase

Demand

Central control view for telecom engineers. Orion allows them to overview key graphics related to data consumption and spending, verify connection profiles and search and purchase new third party venues as required.

Marketing
website

A series of illustrations were developed to help explain the value proposition of Orion. This style is the result of a middle ground between Google Ads and Orion's custom design language. It gives the familiarity of a Google product without sharing the same exact branding style.

Design System

Bimbo Mexico

Design SystemVisual DesignBrandFigma

Context

Bimbo is a Mexican multinational food company with presence all around the American continent, Europe, Asia and Africa. Their main products are related to bakery and snacks which are sold in big supermarket chain stores and small neighborhood shops. This not only offers wide access to customers but a logistic problem as well.

Challenge

To solve the distribution issues, Bimbo created Market Map (Motor Analítico de Potencial de Ventas) — a mobile app whose aim is to help the regular store seller and their supervisors in the work field. After many months of implementation, user feedback has proven that there are several UX and accessibility problems that need to be addressed.

Goal

Develop a design system aligned with Bimbo's brand identity with close consideration to accessibility in open environments and improve the user flow experience.

Project Overview

Bimbo is a Mexican multinational food company with presence all around the American continent, Europe, Asia and Africa. Their main products are related to bakery and snacks which are sold in big supermarket chain stores and small neighborhood shops.

To solve the distribution issues, Bimbo created Market Map (Motor Analítico de Potencial de Ventas) — a mobile app to help store sellers and supervisors in the field. User feedback has proven several UX and accessibility problems that need to be addressed.

Develop a design system aligned with Bimbo's brand identity with close consideration to accessibility in open environments and improve the user flow experience.

Why a Design System?

It is a comprehensive system that contains design principles, components, interaction patterns, organization, and documentation to ensure the consistency and scalability of a digital product or family of products.

Visual consistency and functionality

It sets clear rules for components, ensuring that all interface elements are consistent across the interface.

Team collaboration

Reduce misunderstandings and ensure that all project members work with the same guidelines.

Save time and resources

In the long run, a DS reduces the time spent on design and development decisions.

Design Process

Business Manager

Tracks the demand of Bimbo products in the region and coordinates efforts on the factory output and product advertisement.

Supervisor

Keeps a close look into seller performance and objectives. Organizes the local distribution of products.

Seller

Door-to-door personnel in charge of delivering Bimbo's products to small neighborhood stores around Mexico.

Platform Audit

Full audit revealing inconsistent patterns.

  • Lack of components consistency
  • Improper use of colors according to Bimbo's manual
  • No hierarchy between the interface's elements
  • Poor contrast between components
  • No common visual language in the icon's design

The foundational layer of the Design System — a curated set of tokens for font, spacing, typography, and color that every component is built on.

Color

Semantic color roles for surfaces, content, borders, and states — derived from Bimbo's brand palette and validated for accessibility.

Typography

A defined type scale covering display, heading, body, and caption styles — each with its own size, weight, and line-height.

Spacing

A consistent spacing scale that defines margins, paddings, and layout gaps to keep vertical rhythm uniform across screens.

Border

Border widths and corner radii that shape the edges and rounded corners of every component across the system.

A new graphic identity was created to align with Bimbo's visual language, achieving cohesion between this product and the broader brand universe.

Market MAP Design System

A defined typographic system that establishes hierarchy, readability, and personality across the entire product — chosen to feel close to Bimbo's brand voice while remaining functional in dense data screens.

A custom icon library built specifically for the Design System — over 900 icons drawn with a shared visual language, consistent stroke weight, and clear meaning at every scale.

A semantic color palette derived from Bimbo's brand guidelines — with carefully curated grays for surfaces, content, and borders, validated for contrast and accessibility in real field conditions.

More than 80 production-ready components covering every recurring UI pattern in the product — each with its states, variants, and accessibility specs baked in from the start.

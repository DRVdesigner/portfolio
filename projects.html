AI Chatbot · App Maintenance

Nissan Latam

UX DesignUI DesignAutomotiveAI ChatbotFigma

Project Overview

Led UX and visual design for Nissan's Latin America digital maintenance platform — a unified experience allowing vehicle owners to schedule services, track maintenance history and communicate with dealerships via AI chatbot, across 6 markets simultaneously.

Design Process

Understanding the Market

We audited Nissan's digital touchpoints across 6 LATAM markets — identifying inconsistencies in service booking flows, language gaps, and disconnected dealer systems creating friction for vehicle owners.

  • Competitor benchmarking across 8 automotive brands
  • Stakeholder interviews with regional market leads
  • Heuristic evaluation of the existing platform

User Journey Mapping

We conducted 24 interviews with vehicle owners in Mexico, Brazil and Argentina — mapping the emotional journey from service alert to vehicle pickup. Pain points centered on scheduling uncertainty and dealership communication gaps.

  • 24 user interviews across 3 markets
  • Journey maps & pain point prioritization
  • 3 user archetypes defined

IA & Chatbot Flows

We defined the information architecture for the app and AI chatbot — mapping 30+ conversation trees, service booking flows, and vehicle status modules. Mid-fidelity wireframes were tested with 12 users before visual design began.

  • 30+ chatbot conversation flows architected
  • Mid-fi wireframes validated with 12 users
  • 3 iterative sprint cycles before sign-off

Visual Design & Handoff

Final UI built on Nissan's brand system with a cleaner, accessible visual language tuned for mobile. Delivered 120+ screen designs and a Figma component library ready for engineering handoff across 6 language variants.

  • 120+ screens across 6 languages
  • Component library with design tokens
  • Figma handoff + QA support

02

Design System

Bimbo Mexico

Design SystemVisual DesignBrandFigma

Project Overview

Design studio engagement for Grupo Bimbo's Mexico division — building a scalable design system and visual language for one of the world's largest bakery companies. The system unified 3 digital products under a coherent brand experience.

Design Process

Platform Audit

Full audit of Bimbo's digital touchpoints — e-commerce, B2B portal, and marketing site. The audit revealed 200+ inconsistent UI patterns, 6 different button styles, and no unified typography scale across products.

  • 200+ UI elements catalogued across 3 products
  • Brand guidelines analysis & gap mapping
  • Accessibility review — WCAG 2.1 AA

Design Token Architecture

We defined a full token hierarchy — from primitive values to semantic aliases and component-specific tokens. This enabled both theme flexibility and brand consistency, with full dark mode support included from day one.

  • Color system — 8 semantic roles + dark mode
  • Typography scale — 9 styles, 2 typefaces
  • Spacing, radius, shadow, and motion tokens

Component Library

80+ components built in Figma with full variants, states, and auto-layout — organized into foundations, patterns, and templates following atomic design principles adapted to Bimbo's product complexity.

  • 80+ Figma components with all states
  • Atomic structure — atoms to organisms
  • Interactive prototypes for complex patterns

Guidelines & Handoff

Every component documented with usage rules, do/don't examples, and accessibility notes — delivered as a Figma file and a Notion-based site accessible to designers, developers, and stakeholders alike.

  • Documentation for all 80+ components
  • Dev handoff specs and CSS variable export
  • Onboarding guide for new team members

Dashboard Design · UX Architecture · UI Kit · Illustrations

Orion by Google

DashboardUX ArchitectureUI KitIllustrationsBranding

Project Overview

Led information architecture and complete dashboard design for Orion — Google's internal analytics and monitoring platform. Ensured clarity, visual hierarchy, and optimal usability for data-heavy interfaces used by engineering and product teams daily.

Design Process

Understanding Data Complexity

We worked closely with engineering and product teams to understand the data models — 15+ metric types, 4 monitoring domains, and divergent mental models between technical and non-technical users. This shaped every subsequent architecture decision.

  • Stakeholder workshops with 4 user groups
  • Data taxonomy and metric hierarchy mapping
  • Competitive analysis of monitoring dashboards

Information Architecture

We defined a three-level navigation hierarchy — global overview, domain monitoring, metric drilldown — with contextual filtering. Card sorting sessions with 18 internal users validated the taxonomy before any screens were designed.

  • Card sorting with 18 internal users
  • 3-level navigation hierarchy defined
  • Contextual linking between related metrics

Dashboard Design

We designed the full dashboard system — 40+ screen states across 6 monitoring domains. Every chart type, table, and filter was crafted with data density and visual clarity in mind, tested in 3 rounds of usability sessions.

  • 40+ screens across 6 data domains
  • 8 chart types with responsive variants
  • 3 rounds of usability testing

UI Kit & Illustrations

We delivered a comprehensive UI kit — 60+ components purpose-built for data-heavy interfaces — alongside a custom illustration system for empty states, onboarding, and contextual guidance across the platform.

  • 60+ data-specific components in Figma
  • Custom illustration library — 12 scenes
  • Motion principles and transition specs

Brand Identity · Game UI · Visual Design

La Calera

Brand IdentityGame UIVisual DesignFigma

Project Overview

Complete brand identity and visual design system for La Calera — from logo and color language to game UI components and marketing assets. A fully cohesive visual ecosystem built for a gaming brand launching across digital and physical channels.

Design Process

Brand Strategy & Direction

Deep competitive analysis of gaming brands — identifying positioning gaps and visual territories. Three creative directions were explored, refined through client workshops, and narrowed to a bold, high-contrast identity rooted in energy and precision.

  • Competitive landscape & positioning audit
  • 3 creative directions developed & tested
  • Brand archetypes and voice definition

Logo & Visual Identity

The logo system was developed through 12+ concept explorations — arriving at a mark that works at every scale and context. The color language, typography, and visual motion system were all defined as a unified identity.

  • 12+ logo concepts, 3 presentation rounds
  • Color palette — primary, accent & neutrals
  • Typography — display, UI and body scales

Game UI Design

We designed the complete game UI system — HUD elements, menus, inventory, leaderboards, and onboarding flows. Every component conveys immediacy and immersion while maintaining accessibility for all player types.

  • HUD system with real-time data displays
  • 50+ game UI components across all screens
  • Motion specs for transitions and feedback

Marketing Assets

The brand was extended into marketing channels — social media templates, key art, merchandise guidelines, and digital advertising. A complete brand book was delivered enabling the internal team to manage the system independently.

  • Social media templates — 8 formats
  • Key art and hero visuals for launch
  • Brand book and usage guidelines
